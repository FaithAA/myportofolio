Textile Origami Inspired Research

Experimental research on textile-based material behavior and durability.

Overview

This research investigates how stitched textile structures deform and fold under different geometric configurations. By systematically varying stitch pattern parameters such as width, length, and orientation angle, I studied how these factors influence the resulting folding behavior of the material. The project combines hands-on fabrication, controlled experimentation, and data analysis to better understand how stitched patterns can be used to program mechanical behavior in textile-based systems.

What I Have Worked On So Far

  • Designed stitched textile samples with different width, length, and angle configurations
  • Conducted controlled experiments to measure folding angles and deformation behavior
  • Collected and organized experimental data across multiple test configurations
  • Analyzed the results using Python to compute averages, variability, and visualize patterns in the data
  • Used experimental findings to understand how stitch geometry influences folding response

Tools

Material testing (Instron + Heat guns/Oven), prototyping(Laser Cutting + Sewing), experimental design (Forces analysics), data-driven analysis(Python)
