Dancing Robot

Sound-activated robot built with digital fabrication, sensors, and web control.

Overview

This project involved designing and prototyping a dancing robot that could respond to sound and be controlled through a web-based interface. It combined mechanical design, electronics, and interactive controls.

What I Worked On

  • Designed and fabricated the robot’s structure using 3D printed and laser-cut parts
  • Integrated ESP32, microphones, buzzers, and photoresistors
  • Calibrated sound sensors to trigger robot motion
  • Developed a LAN-based interface for rhythm and song-based control

Tools

ESP32, 3D printing, laser cutting, sensors, digital fabrication
