Dancing Robot

Sound-activated dummy dancer built with digital fabrication, sensors, and web control.

Overview

This personal project involved designing and prototyping a dancing robot that could respond to sound and be controlled through a web-based interface as well as sensors. It combined mechanical design, electronics (sensors and actuators), and interactive controls. I attempted this to better my understanding of circuits and get used to lab environment + tools as well as get familiar with a different set of electronics.

What I Worked On

  • Designed using Fusion360 and fabricated the robot’s structure using 3D printed and laser-cut parts
  • Integrated ESP32, microphones, buzzers, motors, and photoresistors
  • Calibrated sound sensors to trigger robot motion to respond to music
  • Developed a LAN-based interface for rhythm and song-based control as microphones wouldn't distinguish a song from room noise

Tools

ESP32, 3D printing, laser cutting, sensors, digital fabrication
